# Light SAFEMARG website refresh

- Convert the main scrolling SAFEMARG website from dark blue to a clean, professional light palette.
- Keep the perception, 3D simulator, and hardware walkthrough interfaces unchanged.
- Remove SIH, MathWorks, IIT, IEEE, and partner/finalist claims from visible website copy and page metadata.

- Use white and soft-neutral section backgrounds, dark readable text, restrained teal accents, thin borders, and subtle shadows.
- Simplify the first screen, navigation, content sections, cards, buttons, contact area, and footer while preserving the existing layout and actions.
- Replace the affiliation badge row with factual product proof points about Indian-road scenarios, real-time replanning, and simulation-based validation.
- Remove unsupported advisor and partnership references while retaining general customer categories such as OEMs, universities, fleets, and government.
